About

A public translation workspace, not a plate archive.

Read the source transcription and machine-draft English together. Translation suggestions enter a shared public desk; editors review them for later releases. Photographs remain with the institutions that published them.

How to take part

  1. Read freely

    No account. Open manuscripts, machine drafts, source links, and public translation suggestions.

  2. Improve one line

    Sign in with GitHub and suggest clearer or more accurate English. A note is optional.

  3. Review in public

    Editors and trusted reviewers discuss suggestions and record what has been checked.

Hebrew and Aramaic sources

The original-language wording comes from two public Text-Fabric repositories. Both are CC BY-NC 4.0. Full notes: ETCBC/dss and ETCBC/BHSA. How this site uses them: the Sources document in the repo (docs/SOURCES.md).

  • ETCBC/dss

    Diplomatic transcription of the scrolls. Martin G. Abegg, Jr.; CACCHT; Eep Talstra Centre for Bible and Computer. This is the Hebrew, Aramaic, and rare Greek wording on each manuscript page.

    Wording

  • ETCBC/BHSA 2021

    Biblical Hebrew lexeme and gloss features. Eep Talstra Centre for Bible and Computer. Lexical grounding for later translation work. A BHSA gloss is not the English of a Qumran line.

    Lexical grounding

Planned public-domain glosses for this site are BDB 1906 and Jastrow 1903. Leon Levy and the Israel Museum are outbound photograph viewers only. We do not host plates.
